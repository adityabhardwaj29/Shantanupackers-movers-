Step-by-Step Workflow

How Your Move Happens

A structured 5-step methodology ensuring flawless execution on moving day.

01. Survey & Quote

Virtual or physical inventory audit followed by an official, 100% written binding price estimate.

02. 4-Layer Packing

Our uniformed technicians wrap every item in bubble wrap, corner foam, 5-ply cartons, and moisture film.

03. Container Loading

Balanced cargo stacking inside closed weatherproof containers with hydraulic tie-downs.

04. GPS Highway Transit

Live tracking through GPS fleet telemetry and milestone updates to your phone.

05. Setup & Inspection

Unloading into respective rooms, bed/wardrobe reassembly, and joint sign-off delivery inspection.
