Pricing Factors

What Affects Your Moving Cost?

Moving costs depend on several factors. Here's what we consider when calculating your quote.

Distance

Local moves cost less than long-distance intercity relocations. The farther the destination, the higher the transport cost.

Home Size

1 BHK, 2 BHK, 3 BHK or commercial — the number of rooms and items directly affects labour and transport costs.

Floor & Lift Access

Higher floors without a lift require extra manpower and time, which affects the total price slightly.

Packing Level

Full professional packing with premium materials costs more than transport-only or loading-only services.

Moving Date

Peak season (Oct–Feb) and last-minute bookings may have slightly higher rates. Book in advance for best pricing.

Special Items

Pianos, large aquariums, antiques, or heavy gym equipment require special handling and may add to cost.
