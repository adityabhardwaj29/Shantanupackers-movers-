Terms and Conditions

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Shantanu Packers and Movers (UDYAM-MH-17-0244739)

1. Scope & Quotation Validity

1.1. All official written quotations issued by Shantanu Packers and Movers (UDYAM-MH-17-0244739) are based on the initial item inventory survey provided by the customer.
1.2. Written quotations are binding for a period of 15 days from the date of issuance.
1.3. If additional items, heavy furniture, or extra pickup/drop locations not listed in the initial survey are added on moving day, charges will be updated transparently according to standard rate schedules before loading.

2. Customer Responsibilities & Restricted Items

2.1. Customers are strictly prohibited from packing or consigning the following items in moving containers:
• Cash, currency, promissory notes, bank cheques, and credit cards.
• Gold, diamond, or precious jewelry, gemstones, and high-value watches.
• Inflammable liquids, petrol, diesel, gas cylinders, fireworks, acids, or hazardous chemicals.
• Perishable food items and plants during intercity journeys.
2.2. The customer is advised to carry personal identity documents, property papers, passports, and essential medications in their personal baggage.

3. 4-Layer Packaging & Safety Assurance

3.1. Shantanu Packers and Movers utilizes high-grade virgin air bubble wrap, foam corner protectors, 5-ply corrugated cartons, and moisture-proof stretch wrap.
3.2. All fragile glass items, LED/OLED televisions, and electronics are crated or cushioned using industry-standard protective materials.
3.3. Customer agrees to allow our trained packing crew full access to wrap and secure goods according to safety protocols.

4. Payment Terms & Schedule

4.1. Booking Advance: 10% to 20% at the time of confirming the moving date slot.
4.2. Loading & Dispatch: 70% upon completion of packing and loading into the container truck at origin.
4.3. Final Settlement: Remaining balance upon arrival and prior to final unloading and placement at destination.
4.4. Payments can be made via UPI, Bank NEFT/RTGS, or corporate cheque (for GST accounts).

5. Transit Insurance & Claims

5.1. Comprehensive transit insurance (covering accidental vehicle damage or overturn in transit) is arranged through national insurance partners upon customer declaration.
5.2. Any transit damage or discrepancy must be noted on the physical/digital Consignment Note (Bilty) at the exact time of delivery and counter-signed by the moving supervisor.
5.3. Claims must be formally submitted in writing with photographic evidence within 48 hours of delivery.

6. Cancellation & Rescheduling

6.1. Rescheduling requests made at least 24 hours prior to the scheduled packing time will be accommodated at no additional fee, subject to vehicle slot availability.
6.2. In case of cancellation after a crew has already arrived at the origin location, a nominal mobilization charge will be applicable.

7. Legal Jurisdiction

7.1. Any disputes arising under or in connection with the relocation services shall be governed by the laws of India and subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts in Palghar / Vasai, Maharashtra.
