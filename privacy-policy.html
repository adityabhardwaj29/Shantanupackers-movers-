DATA PROTECTION & PRIVACY NOTICE

Privacy Policy

How we protect, handle, and securely manage your personal and relocation information.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a quote request or contact form on our website, we collect your full name, phone number, email address, pickup location, destination address, and item inventory details. This information is gathered solely to provide shifting quotations, coordinate packing crews, and arrange logistics.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your personal information is strictly used for: (1) Generating accurate shifting quotes, (2) Assigning on-site supervisors and vehicles, (3) Sending booking confirmation and driver contact details via SMS/WhatsApp, and (4) Facilitating transit insurance documentation.

3. Zero Spam & No Data Sharing

We strictly enforce a zero-spam policy. Shantanu Packers and Movers does not sell, rent, or lease your phone number or personal details to any 3rd party marketing agencies or telemarketers.

4. Grievance Officer & Contact

If you have questions regarding our privacy practices or wish to request data deletion, contact our Data Grievance Desk at shantanupackers@gmail.com or call +91 8218059678.
