Privacy Policy

How Shantanu Packers and Movers collects, protects, and handles your personal and relocation information.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit an inquiry or book a move with Shantanu Packers and Movers, we collect your name, phone number, email address, pickup location, drop location, inventory specifications, and moving dates solely for quotation and logistics dispatch purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is strictly utilized for calculating binding estimates, communicating move status, coordinating driver/loader arrivals, generating GST relocation invoices, and providing post-move customer support.

3. Data Security Guarantee

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party marketing companies. Data is protected with industry-standard encryption protocols and secure database access.

4. Contacting Our Grievance Officer

For questions regarding our privacy practices or to request data modification, contact us at: shantanupackers@gmail.com or call +91 8218059678.
