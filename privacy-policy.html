Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Shantanu Packers and Movers (UDYAM-MH-17-0244739)

1. Overview & Commitment

At Shantanu Packers and Movers (UDYAM-MH-17-0244739), we value your trust. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, handle, utilize, and protect your personal information when you request a quotation, book a relocation, track a container shipment, or communicate with our operations team.

2. Information We Collect

When you interact with our website or booking desks, we may collect:
• Contact Information: Full name, phone number, alternate phone number, and email address.
• Location Data: Pickup address, floor number, lift availability, destination delivery address, and landmark details.
• Inventory Specifications: List of furniture, home appliances, vehicles, and special delicate packing requests.
• Tracking & Communication Logs: WhatsApp chat transcripts, quote requests, and consignment feedback.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to:
• Calculate and provide an accurate, written 100% binding price quotation.
• Dispatch appropriate vehicle sizes (14ft, 17ft, 19ft, 22ft containers) and allocate packing crews.
• Send real-time milestone notifications via SMS/WhatsApp regarding vehicle dispatch, transit updates, and delivery confirmation.
• Generate legal GST tax invoices and arrange transit insurance coverage.

4. Strict Non-Disclosure Policy

We DO NOT sell, rent, lease, or trade your personal contact details to any third-party marketing companies or lead aggregators. Your contact details are accessed exclusively by our internal dispatch supervisors and assigned packing crew leads.

5. Data Protection & Security

All electronic booking records and consignment information are stored securely using industry-standard encryption protocols. Physical consignment copies are retained only as required for statutory MSME and GST compliance under Indian law.
